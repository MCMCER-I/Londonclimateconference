The Imperative

Strengthening London’s Climate Resilience

Extreme heat is no longer a distant climate scenario, it is a growing challenge for London’s buildings, infrastructure, workplaces and communities.

With around one million London homes potentially at high risk of overheating, and heat-related impacts already affecting productivity, public services and infrastructure, the need for practical adaptation is becoming increasingly important.

The London Climate Resilience Roundtable 2026 brings together perspectives from business, the built environment, infrastructure, academia and sustainability to examine how London can prepare for rising temperatures and strengthen resilience through practical, collaborative approaches.

Cross-Sector Perspectives

Bringing together professionals and subject-matter experts from business, infrastructure, the built environment, academia and sustainability to examine London’s climate resilience from different perspectives.

Practical Climate Adaptation

Focused discussions on real challenges facing London, including heat-resilient buildings and infrastructure, retrofitting, workforce resilience, corporate adaptation and climate disclosure.

Focused Expert Dialogue

Two parallel roundtables provide a structured setting for participants to share experience, exchange perspectives and explore practical approaches to the challenges of extreme heat and climate adaptation.

Shared Outcomes

Insights, key findings and practical solutions emerging from the discussions will be consolidated into a post-event report and shared with relevant government bodies and participating stakeholders..
